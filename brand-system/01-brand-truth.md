Playful · Bold · Cheeky · Authentic · Creative · Social

## 5. Visual Identity (dari logo rasmi "Doh-Nut")

| Elemen | Spesifikasi |
|---|---|
| Red badge | `#EF233C` — blob utama, CTA, aksen |
| White/cream lettering | Putih dengan outline navy |
| Navy outline | `#1D3557` — teks, border, ink |
| Blue outer border | Border luar logo |
| Background kuning | `#FDE047` — signature bg app & assets |
| Butter cream | `#FFF9DB` — surface lembut |
| Bentuk | Rounded / playful forms, bold & approachable |

### Palet Token (web)

```css
--background: #FDE047;   /* kuning logo */
--foreground: #1D3557;   /* navy outline */
--primary:    #EF233C;   /* merah badge */
--cream:      #FFF9DB;   /* butter */
```

Contrast: navy atas kuning ≈ 9.9:1 (AAA) · putih atas merah ≈ 4.6:1 (AA bold).

## 6. Inspirasi "Play-Doh" (feeling, bukan copy)

> Ambil **feeling of play, tactile creativity dan dough manipulation** — BUKAN identiti visual atau trademark Play-Doh.

Konsep: tactile, squishy, rounded, colourful, moldable, playful, experimental, creative.

Dough boleh: **shape · twist · fill · dip · sprinkle · stack · squish · share**

Mental model:

# **MAKE IT. PLAY IT. EAT IT. SHARE IT.**

## 7. Brand Promise & Audience

- **Promise:** Donut premium yang menggembirakan, dihantar fresh.
- **Audience:** Youth / internet-native / Klang Valley, penggemar food-hype & streetwear culture.

## 8. Strategi High-End (penyerapan prinsip)

| Rujukan | Prinsip Diambil |

# **PREMIUM VISUAL + STUPIDLY FUNNY COPY**

Visual: cinematic, editorial, premium, controlled.
Copy: funny, cheeky, short, internet-native, memorable.
