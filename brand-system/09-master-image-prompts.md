## 01 — KUIH BURGER MALAYSIA

Create a premium commercial food advertising photograph for DOH-NUT, a bold, youthful Malaysian doughnut brand.

HERO PRODUCT:
One single freshly fried DOH-NUT made from an ultra-soft, fluffy Malaysian doughnut dough. The doughnut is golden-brown on the outside with a delicate fried surface and an extremely soft, airy, pillowy interior.

The DOH-NUT is sliced horizontally and opened like a Malaysian street-food burger.

FILLING:
Generous glossy Malaysian sambal ikan bilis filling inside the doughnut.
Visible crispy fried ikan bilis layered throughout the sambal.
Fresh thin cucumber slices.
Fresh crisp green salad leaves.

The filling must look generous, authentic, appetising and naturally packed inside the soft doughnut.

VISUAL CHARACTER:
The DOH-NUT must look extremely soft and fluffy, never hard, dense, dry or chewy.
Show realistic bread pores and airy interior.
The contrast between the soft dough, glossy sambal, crispy ikan bilis, fresh cucumber and salad must be visually obvious.

COMPOSITION:
One hero DOH-NUT only.
Three-quarter close-up product angle.
Slightly opened so the filling is clearly visible.
Premium commercial food photography.
Strong hero-product focus.
Minimal modern Malaysian studio environment.
Clean composition with subtle depth of field.

LIGHTING:
Warm cinematic food lighting.
Soft directional highlights.
Natural shadows.
Rich golden-brown tones.
Premium editorial food photography quality.
Highly realistic food texture.

STYLE:
Photorealistic.
Luxury food campaign.
Modern Malaysian street-food identity.
Bold, youthful and appetising.
High-end advertising photography.
Ultra-detailed realistic dough and filling textures.

DO NOT include:
kerepek, chips, sesame seeds, cheese, egg, mayonnaise, ketchup, burger bun, utensils, plates, packaging, text, typography, watermark, extra doughnuts or multiple products.

## 02 — SIRA KUIH KERIA

Create a premium commercial food advertising photograph for DOH-NUT, a bold, youthful Malaysian doughnut brand.

HERO PRODUCT:
One single freshly fried DOH-NUT made from an ultra-soft, fluffy Malaysian doughnut dough.

The DOH-NUT has a beautiful golden-brown fried exterior and an extremely soft, airy, pillowy interior.

SIRA:
The entire DOH-NUT is coated with authentic Malaysian gula Melaka sira inspired by traditional Kuih Keria.

The gula Melaka sira must form a thin, delicate crystallized sugar coating around the doughnut.
The coating should appear amber-brown, slightly glossy and naturally crystallized, with subtle fine sugar crystals and a delicate crackly shell.

IMPORTANT:
This is a SIRA coating, NOT a liquid gula Melaka drizzle.
The sugar coating must visibly cling to and cover the DOH-NUT.
It should look lightly caramelised, crystallized and slightly crackly while preserving the soft dough underneath.

VISUAL CHARACTER:
Extremely soft fluffy dough underneath.
Thin crisp crystallized gula Melaka shell outside.
Clear visual contrast between soft interior and delicate crunchy sugar coating.

COMPOSITION:
One hero DOH-NUT only.
Three-quarter close-up product angle.
Premium commercial food photography.
Minimal modern Malaysian studio environment.
Clean composition.
Strong product focus.
Subtle depth of field.

LIGHTING:
Warm cinematic lighting.
Soft golden highlights reflecting naturally from the gula Melaka coating.
Rich amber tones.
Natural shadows.
Premium editorial food photography.

STYLE:
Photorealistic.
Luxury food campaign.
Modern Malaysian identity.
Traditional Kuih Keria inspiration reimagined as DOH-NUT.
Bold, youthful and premium.
Ultra-realistic dough texture.
Highly detailed crystallized sugar texture.

DO NOT include:
coconut, sesame seeds, chocolate, cream, icing, liquid syrup dripping heavily, chocolate sauce, sprinkles, utensils, plates, packaging, text, typography, watermark, extra doughnuts or multiple products.

## 03 — SIRA SAMBAL

Create a premium commercial food advertising photograph for DOH-NUT, a bold, youthful Malaysian doughnut brand.

HERO PRODUCT:
One single freshly fried DOH-NUT made from an ultra-soft, fluffy Malaysian doughnut dough.

The DOH-NUT has a delicate golden-brown fried exterior and an extremely soft, airy, pillowy interior.

SIRA SAMBAL:
The entire DOH-NUT is coated with authentic Malaysian sambal sira pedas-manis.

The sambal coating must be rich deep red, glossy, sticky and lacquered, clinging naturally to the surface of the doughnut.

The coating should look cooked down into a thick sambal sira — concentrated, slightly caramelised and intensely flavourful.

FINISH:
Generous toasted sesame seeds naturally scattered over the sambal coating.

The toasted sesame must provide visible texture and a subtle golden contrast against the deep red sambal.

IMPORTANT:
The sesame seeds are the ONLY crunchy topping.
NO kerepek.
NO chips.
NO crushed crackers.

VISUAL CHARACTER:
Extremely soft and fluffy dough underneath.
Glossy sticky sambal sira coating outside.
Toasted sesame providing light crunch and texture.
The product should look spicy, savoury, sweet, sticky and intensely Malaysian.

COMPOSITION:
One hero DOH-NUT only.
Three-quarter close-up product angle.
Premium commercial food advertising photography.
Strong hero-product focus.
Minimal modern Malaysian studio environment.
Clean composition.
Subtle depth of field.

LIGHTING:
Dramatic warm cinematic food lighting.
Strong highlights on the glossy sambal coating.
Deep rich red and warm golden tones.
Natural shadows.
Premium editorial food photography.

STYLE:
Photorealistic.
Luxury food campaign.
Modern Malaysian street-food identity.
Traditional Malaysian sambal sira technique reimagined as DOH-NUT.
Bold, youthful and premium.
Ultra-realistic food texture.
Highly detailed glossy sambal and toasted sesame texture.

DO NOT include:
kerepek, chips, crackers, ikan bilis, cucumber, salad, coconut, cheese, mayonnaise, cream, chocolate, utensils, plates, packaging, text, typography, watermark, extra doughnuts or multiple products.

### VISUAL RULE UNTUK 3 FLAVOUR

Satu konsep → satu bentuk DOH-NUT → satu visual language.

| Flavour                  | Hero treatment                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kuih Burger Malaysia | DOH-NUT dibuka + filling                                  |
| Sira Kuih Keria      | DOH-NUT full coated + crystallized gula Melaka            |
| Sira Sambal          | DOH-NUT full coated + glossy sambal sira + toasted sesame |

Ini memastikan bila ketiga-tiga gambar diletakkan sebelah-sebelah, ia nampak seperti satu brand / satu product family, bukan tiga konsep makanan yang berbeza.
